From Earth science to action

Start with mechanisms and observations, not slogans. A strong learning path distinguishes weather from climate, tracks feedbacks, connects biodiversity to ecosystems, and tests actions against trade-offs.

Use maps, stories, data, and local observations to describe what is changing and where.

Learn from trusted starting points

Use age-appropriate explanations first, then move toward structured courses, primary data, and local projects.

NASA Climate Kids

Free

NASA's student resource for grades K–8 uses games, hands-on activities, videos, articles, and interactive media to explain climate science through Earth-observing missions.

Explore NASA Climate Kids

UN CC:Learn

Free courses

United Nations climate-learning courses cover climate change, green economy, and circular economy. The catalog includes self-paced and moderated courses, with certification terms shown per course.

Browse UN courses

National Geographic Education

Free resources

Filterable classroom resources include live events, maps, videos, interactives, and materials across geography, social studies, and biology for multiple grade bands.

Free

Explore age-friendly explainers on the greenhouse effect, carbon cycle, climate change, and connections between oceans and climate.
