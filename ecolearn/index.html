Key Learning Features

Observe

Use maps, stories, data, and local observations to describe what is changing and where.

Explain

Connect greenhouse effect, water, energy, ecosystems, and human decisions without losing nuance.

Act and evaluate

Choose a practical action, define who benefits, and revisit the result rather than treating action as a one-time gesture.

Climate Simulations

Interactive models to predict environmental changes and test solutions.

Citizen Science

Join real-world projects to collect data on wildlife and pollution.
