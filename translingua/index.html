Key Translation Features

Identify

Is it text, speech, camera input, a document, a web page, or a live group conversation?

Draft

Use a tool that supports the needed mode, then preserve the source and note uncertainty.

Verify

Check names, numbers, negation, tone, cultural context, and sensitive terminology before sharing.

Offline Translation

Download supported language packs for offline translation when connectivity is limited. Check the language and mode on your device before relying on it while traveling.

Multi-Person Conversation

Some apps support multi-device or multi-person translated conversations, live captions, or transcription. Confirm the supported languages, speaking modes, and privacy settings first.

Tap to Translate

On supported devices and apps, copying text can open a quick translation action without switching workflows. Availability depends on the operating system, app, and account settings.

World's Most Popular Translator

  • Text, camera, speech, document, and website modes with language coverage that varies by feature

  • Document translation with supported file types and formatting limits

Google Translate is useful for everyday drafts and quick access, but review complex, specialized, or high-stakes content. For product integrations, use the separate Google Cloud Translation service and review its current terms, quotas, and data controls.
