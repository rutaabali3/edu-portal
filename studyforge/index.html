The study loop

Capture, schedule, retrieve

Productivity is not the same as learning. Use planning to protect time, notes to create material, and retrieval to test what remains available without looking.

01

Capture

Collect assignments, lecture ideas, questions, deadlines, and references in a trusted home.

Reduce friction
02

Schedule

Turn large projects into the next visible action and place it on a realistic day.

Make time
03

Retrieve

Close the notes and answer questions, solve problems, or teach the idea from memory.

Choose by job to be done

Do not add an app because it is popular. Add it when it solves a specific bottleneck in your learning workflow.

Todoist

Free + Pro

Capture tasks with natural language, organize Today and Upcoming, schedule due dates, set recurring work, and use student-planning templates. The free plan has limits.

Explore Todoist

Notion for Education

Eligible students

Student planners, notes, databases, calendars, websites, job applications, and club workspaces. The free Education Plus plan requires eligible higher-education verification.

Platform-dependent

Spaced-repetition flashcards, media support, customizable reviews, and free AnkiWeb synchronization. Desktop and mobile availability differs by platform.

Questions beginners ask
