Key Productivity Features

Capture

Collect assignments, lecture ideas, questions, deadlines, and references in a trusted home.

Schedule

Turn large projects into the next visible action and place it on a realistic day.

Retrieve

Close the notes and answer questions, solve problems, or teach the idea from memory.

Gamification

Earn rewards and badges for completing tasks to stay motivated.

Smart Reminders

Get notifications across devices to never miss a deadline or review.

Offline Sync

Access your notes and tasks without internet and sync when back online.

Try Notion

Ultimate All-in-One Workspace

Notion is the favorite free tool for over 20 million students worldwide. It combines notes, tasks, databases, and wikis into one flexible platform for organized studying.

  • Customizable templates for study plans
  • Real-time collaboration with classmates
  • Embedded calendars and to-do lists
  • Offline editing with auto-sync
  • Free for personal and educational use
