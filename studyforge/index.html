Popular Productivity Tools

Todoist

Capture tasks with natural language, organize Today and Upcoming, schedule due dates, set recurring work, and use integrations. The free Beginner plan currently includes 5 personal projects, 3 filter views, reminders, and 1 week of activity history; calendar layout and broader limits are Pro features.

Free + Pro Web & Mobile Task Management

Notion for Education

Student planners, notes, databases, calendars, websites, job applications, and club workspaces. Eligible higher-education students and teachers can receive a free Education Plus plan for a one-member workspace with a school email; student organizations have a separate offer.

Eligible students Desktop & Mobile Flashcards

Anki

Spaced-repetition flashcards, media support, customizable reviews, and free AnkiWeb synchronization. Desktop and Android apps are free/open source, while AnkiMobile is a separate paid iOS app; keep backups and sync regularly.

Platform-dependent Mobile Focus Timer

Quizlet

Create or discover flashcards, study guides, practice tests, games, and lecture-based materials. Quizlet Plus plans currently place limits on practice tests, Learn rounds, and textbook solutions, while Plus Unlimited removes those limits; free access varies by feature and account.

Free + paid Web & Mobile Note-Taking

Focus block

Choose one outcome, silence avoidable notifications, work for a bounded interval, and take a real break. A paper timer is a complete tool; Forest can add a game-like timer, but it cannot guarantee focus or replace good study design.

No app required Web & Mobile Quizzes

Exam review

Mix practice questions, worked examples, error logs, and cumulative review. Use retrieval and spaced review to decide what returns to the schedule, then check answers against reliable course materials.

Self-directed Desktop & Web Pomodoro

Key Productivity Features

Capture

Collect assignments, lecture ideas, questions, deadlines, and references in a trusted home.

Schedule

Turn large projects into the next visible action and place it on a realistic day.

Retrieve

Close the notes and answer questions, solve problems, or teach the idea from memory.

Gamification

Optional rewards can make progress visible, but motivation systems work best when paired with a clear next action and realistic workload.

Smart Reminders

Use reminders across devices when available, but keep a weekly review so a missed notification does not become a missed deadline or review.

Offline Sync

Offline access depends on the app and device. Notion supports offline page work in its desktop and mobile apps, while browser behavior and sync timing can vary; test the pages you need before travel.

Try Notion

Flexible workspace with education eligibility

Notion combines notes, tasks, databases, calendars, and wikis in one workspace. Eligible higher-education students and teachers can apply for a free Education Plus plan using an institution email; it is not a universal free student offer.

  • Customizable templates for study plans
  • Sharing and collaboration with classmates; workspace member limits depend on the plan
  • Embedded calendars and to-do lists
  • Offline editing in the desktop and mobile apps; sync when connected
  • Free plan for personal use; Education Plus eligibility is verified
Try Notion Now

Notion’s Free plan is available for personal work, while eligible higher-education students and teachers can receive free Education Plus for a one-member workspace with up to 100 guests and unlimited uploads. Student organizations have separate eligibility; AI and other advanced features may require a paid plan.
