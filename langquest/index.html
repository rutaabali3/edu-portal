Choose the missing piece

These platforms solve different learning problems. Use plan labels as a starting point, not a promise that every feature is available for every language.

Duolingo

Free + paid

Short, gamified lessons for reading, writing, listening, and speaking practice. Course availability, lesson depth, and premium features vary by language and plan.

Browse Duolingo courses

Memrise

Free + paid

Practice useful words and phrases with native-speaker video, pronunciation, sentence building, listening, and AI speaking practice. Paid plans unlock extra features.

Explore Memrise

HelloTalk

Free to start

Language exchange through text, voice, video, Voicerooms, translation, pronunciation, transliteration, and corrections with native speakers.

Talk to the world

Anki

Platform-dependent

Spaced-repetition flashcards schedule reviews for when you are most likely to forget. AnkiWeb sync is free; desktop and mobile availability differ by platform.

Practical answers

Questions beginners ask

A single app can help you build a routine, but fluency needs varied input and output. Add reading, listening, speaking, and writing as your goals expand.

Make cards short and meaningful, include context or audio when helpful, and suspend cards that are ambiguous or irrelevant. Review consistently rather than cramming.

Keep early conversations on-platform, avoid sharing sensitive personal details, use block/report tools, and stop an interaction that becomes uncomfortable. Minors should follow a trusted adult's guidance.
