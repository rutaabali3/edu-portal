Try Duolingo

The World's Most Popular Language App

Duolingo has over 500 million users learning through bite-sized, science-backed lessons. It's free, fun, and effective for beginners to advanced learners.

  • 40+ languages including rare ones like Esperanto
  • Gamified streaks and daily goals
  • AI-powered speaking practice
  • Stories for reading comprehension
  • Free with optional Super Duolingo
Try Duolingo Now

Duolingo is free forever with ads; Super removes ads and adds offline access for a fee.
